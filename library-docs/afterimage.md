---
looks: Lightning bolts that leave a glowing afterimage along their path, fading over a second or two.
---
**lightning** strikes at random moments and **trails** keeps an afterglow of each bolt (30 % left a second later), so
the forks stay printed on the strands after the flash. On sparse strands a bolt jumps from wire to wire; the
afterglow joins the jumps up. **storm** puts the bolts in clouds; **trails** works the same after any moving source.

## Overrides
- trails.decay: how much glow is left after a second; 0.1 a short echo, 0.6 long smears.
- lightning.speed: how often it strikes; lightning.colours the bolt's colours.

## Pairings
- Over a dark colour-wash bed layer (brightness 0.2) for a night sky.
- With **storm** on another layer for strikes out of the clouds.

## Pitfalls
- A long decay with frequent strikes fills the rig with old bolts and loses the flash.
