---
looks: Lightning bolts that leave a glowing afterimage along their path, fading over a second or two.
---
The afterglow keeps each bolt's forks printed on the strands and joins up bolts that jump between sparse wires.
**storm** puts bolts in clouds; **trails** alone works after any moving source.

## Overrides
- trails.decay: glow left after a second; 0.1 a short echo, 0.6 long smears.
- lightning.speed: how often it strikes.

## Pairings
- Over a dark **colour-wash** bed (brightness 0.2) for a night sky.

## Pitfalls
- Long decay with frequent strikes fills the rig with old bolts and loses the flash.
