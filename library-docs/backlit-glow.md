---
looks: Warm stained glass with soft light drifting behind it, like clouds past a sunlit window.
---
**clouds** in white drift behind **stained-glass** on the `centre` rule (heart, middle and rim each a colour of the
ember palette), with a low backlight, so the glass brightens where a cloud passes and dims between. A living window
that never flashes: an ambient bed, a dawn, a quiet moment. **gothic-jewel** is a still jewelled window;
**cathedral-sun** sweeps a band of sunlight.

## Overrides
- clouds.speed: how fast the light drifts; clouds.cover how much of the window is lit.
- stained-glass.colours: the glass, heart to rim; stained-glass.rule: any rule.
- stained-glass.backlight: higher keeps the dim panes brighter between clouds.

## Pairings
- On the `rose`; the look's palette sets the glass (it brings `ember` if the look has none).

## Pitfalls
- On a layer wider than the rose, the glass falls back to angle and distance and paints every group: target the rose.
