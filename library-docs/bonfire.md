**fire** drawn in grey, its **smoke** curling up the arch legs, then **recolour** maps the greys: dim smoke lands on
browns, bright flame on reds and yellows. So smoke and flame come from one source and stay in step. Speeds up with
energy. A campfire, a ritual moment, or a warm slow opener that becomes a blaze.

## Overrides
- fire.height: how far the flames reach.
- fire.speed: a number fixes how fast they flicker.
- smoke.fade: smoke length.
- smoke.curl: draught; 0 rises straight, 1 billows.
- recolour.colours: the smoke and flame colours, dark to bright.

## Pairings
- With **rising-embers** above it for sparks going up.
- **brushfire** on the arches and bonfire on the towers.

## Pitfalls
- fire.colours stay black to white: the colour comes from the recolour.
