**wildfire** in ember colours, its **smoke** drifting up each strand, over a deep red **colour-wash**. Fronts catch,
run, meet and gutter out; new sparks come more often as energy rises. Turned low (spread 0.35, speed 8) it is a
quiet smoulder of dim patches, a living bed.

## Overrides
- wildfire.spread: 0.35 smoulders in patches; 0.65 and up runs in fronts.
- wildfire.speed: how fast fronts travel, LEDs a second.
- wildfire.sparks: a number fixes how often fires start.
- smoke.drift: how far the smoke rises.
- smoke.fade: short wisps or long plumes.

## Pairings
- Over **bonfire** on the towers for a whole burning set.
- A dim **colour-wash** layer beneath keeps the burnt-out wiring warm.

## Pitfalls
- Wildfire spreads along wires: on loose points (spirelets, floods) it only sparks.
