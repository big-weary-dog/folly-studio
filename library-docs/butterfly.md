**plasma** folded by **mirror**: busy, trippy and symmetrical; the towers and portal sides move as mirror images.
**palette-drift** is the calm version; **kaleidoscope** and **tunnel** fold into more than two.

## Overrides
- bars.bars: 8 broad blobs per wing, 16 busy marbling (the maximum).
- bars.speed: 0.1 calm, 0.6 churning.
- turbulence.amount: how far the bands swirl.
- mirror.height: also fold top onto bottom, four-way.
- mirror.depth: fold front onto back.

## Pairings
- As the whole-rig bed with a sparse **sparkle** or **starfield** layer above.
- **hue-shift** after the mirror (speed 0.05) to keep colours travelling.
- **anchor** at the end (tag `rose`) to fold round the rose's centre line.

## Pitfalls
- The fold is round the layer's middle: on an off-centre group the seam lands wrong.
- Turbulence size below ~0.15 speckles on sparse groups.
