A **heartbeat** on every beat, cut to one half by **edge-fade**, turned half a turn every beat by **rotate** (its
angle on a square LFO in beats), so the lit half swaps sides; over a dim **colour-wash**. For a groove where the
crowd starts to sway, or a back-and-forth vocal hook.

## Overrides
- heartbeat.sharpness: 0.3 a swell, 0.9 a knock.
- heartbeat.colours: the thump.
- edge-fade.width: how soft the split is.
- rotate.angle: the LFO's rate is the swap (0.5 every beat, 0.25 every two).

## Pairings
- With **out-of-step**, or over **level-meter**.

## Pitfalls
- The turn is round the layer's centre: on a layer that isn't symmetric the halves differ in size.
