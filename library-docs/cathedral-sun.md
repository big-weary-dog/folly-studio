A morning, a revelation, a slow build. **gothic-jewel** glows pane by pane instead; **backlit-glow** drifts clouds
behind the glass.

## Overrides
- stained-glass.seed: another arrangement of panes.
- stained-glass.speed: how fast the sun crosses (0.2 is one pass in five seconds).
- stained-glass.motion: `glow` instead of the sweep.

## Pairings
- The rest of the Folly in a dim warm **colour-wash**.

## Pitfalls
- Target the `rose`; on a wider layer the glass paints every group by angle and distance.
