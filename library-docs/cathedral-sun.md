**stained-glass** on the `random` rule in jewel colours, with a band of light that sweeps slowly across the window and
brightens each pane it crosses. A morning, a revelation, a slow build. **gothic-jewel** glows pane by pane instead;
**backlit-glow** drifts clouds behind the glass.

## Overrides
- stained-glass.seed: another arrangement of the panes; stained-glass.colours: the glass.
- stained-glass.speed: how fast the sun crosses (0.2 is one pass in five seconds).
- stained-glass.motion: `glow` instead of the sweep.

## Pairings
- On the `rose`; the rest of the Folly in a dim warm **colour-wash**.

## Pitfalls
- On a layer wider than the rose, the glass falls back to angle and distance and paints every group: target the rose.
