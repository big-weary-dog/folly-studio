Fast **wildfire** in the palette with a short **trails** afterglow, its hues spread up the rig by **hue-shift**, over
a dim **colour-wash**. Peak time. With spread near 0.45 and a long rest it turns to avalanches: long calm, then
cascades of every size.

## Overrides
- wildfire.sparks: busy; a number fixes it.
- wildfire.spread: 0.45 avalanches, 0.8 a crowded net.
- wildfire.speed: 20 creeps, 50 races.
- trails.decay: afterglow.
- hue-shift.spread: how much the hue changes with height.

## Pairings
- After **brushfire** for the same idea at full energy.

## Pitfalls
- Wildfire spreads along wires: on loose points it only sparks.
