Light climbs each group of the tag from its own foot to its own top behind a bright head of the last colour, flares as
a crown at the top, then its foot rises after it and the column lifts away. The groups start one after another in
rig order, so towers, arches or petals go up in turn whatever their height. Colour by age cools the light behind the
head back through Colours; along runs Colours along each strand instead. Reach for **bars** with one bar on `up` for
one sweep up the whole layer, **groups-in-turn** for groups lighting in turn without a climb, **shockwave** for a
single burst.

## Params
- tag: `arch` sends the five main and the mini arches up one by one; `all` climbs every smallest group.
- stagger: 0 climbs them all together; 1 spreads the starts over a whole rise, so one group starts as another ends.
- head: 0.04–0.1; larger washes the column toward the last colour.

## Pairings
- Over a dim `colour-wash` bed on `tower`, `spire` or `arch`.
- Add `sparkle` in `high` above for glitter on the rising columns.
- Bind speed to the `energy` global for a quickening ascent at a build.

## Pitfalls
- LEDs in no group of the tag climb together as one; pick a tag the layer's groups carry (`all` always works).
- The order is the rig's, not left to right; mirror or a second layer if it should run the other way.
- The crown flashes the top of each group once a rise; at speed 1 on big groups keep level moderate.
