A **strobe** on the beat over a dim **colour-wash**, then **delay** holds each height a little later than the one
below, in steps, so each flash climbs. Each LED flashes once a beat but the light moves, so it reads as motion. For
the last bars of a build or a four-on-the-floor peak.

## Overrides
- delay.longest: climb time; 0.2 snaps up, 0.6 crawls.
- delay.steps: floors; 0 smooth, 3–8 blocky.
- delay.bounce: meet in the middle.
- strobe.colours: the flash colour.
- strobe.speed: hits in beats per; 1 every beat.

## Pairings
- Over **level-meter** or any bed at a peak.

## Pitfalls
- A longer climb, or shorter flashes, splits each hit into several on the rig: the flash check may refuse it.
