Palette-coloured pleats part from the middle to reveal a wash, every eight seconds. Theatre, reveals, the start of a
set; reads as a proscenium on the arches and portal. **edge-fade** alone wipes without cloth; **rose-bloom** opens
from a centre.

## Overrides
- edge-fade.position: 0.5 shut, 1 open. Bind to the clip envelope (`from` 0.5 `to` 1) to open once; a number holds
  it part-open.
- edge-fade.width: the edge; raise on sparse groups.
- bars.bars: pleats; 6 broad drapes, 20 fine.
- colour-wash.colours: what is revealed.

## Pairings
- To reveal another layer: drop the colour-wash and put the curtain on a layer above it.

## Pitfalls
- Over 20 pleats flickers on sparse LEDs.
