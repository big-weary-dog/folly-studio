Still **clouds** in grey, **recolour**ed through a sunrise and revealed from the ground up by **edge-fade**, over a
near-black **colour-wash**, with **sparkle** stars that go out as the light comes. Everything follows the preset's
`dawn` macro (0 night, 1 full gold; it starts at 0.3). A show opener: give it an automation lane `dawn` from 0 to 1
over the first minute or two, or ride the fader live.

## Overrides
- clouds.speed: drift; 0.03 is barely moving.
- clouds.size: scale of the cloud banks.
- recolour.colours: the sunrise, night to gold, dark to bright.
- sparkle.density: how many stars.
- colour-wash.brightness: the night sky's glow.

## Pairings
- Under a **sparkle** or **fireflies** layer for a crowded night.
- Follow it with **bonfire** or a bright bed once the sun is up.

## Pitfalls
- A preset can't bring an automation lane: draw the `dawn` lane, or nothing rises.
- The edge-fade, recolour and cloud cover are bound to `dawn`; a number there freezes that part of the sunrise.
