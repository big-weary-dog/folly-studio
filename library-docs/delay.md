looks: Lateness that rolls across the rig: a flash becomes a sweep, motion smears like slit-scan.

Each LED shows what is above it a little later than the one before, along Direction, up to Longest seconds at the far
side. A whole-rig **strobe** flash becomes a sweep, a **lightning** bolt replays arch by arch, **sparkle** and
**fireworks** travel. **in-turn** gives whole groups their own clocks instead; **scroll** moves the picture in space,
not time.

## Params
- direction: `up` for rising sweeps; `out` from the rose; `along` runs down every strand.
- longest: 0.2–0.6 s sweeps a flash; 2–4 s smears motion into slow, liquid shapes.
- steps: 3–8 divisions makes blocks that go in turn; N divisions gives N + 1 delay levels including zero. 0 is smooth.
- bounce: 1 starts at both ends and meets in the middle.

## Pairings
- strobe → delay `up`, longest 0.4: a flash climbing the rig on every beat.
- lightning → delay `along`, longest 1: each bolt runs down its wires.

## Pitfalls
- It ends a stage: warps below it don't bend what is above; put warps above it.
- A still or slow pattern shows almost no change: it needs something that changes in time.
- Seeking back or jumping more than half a second starts it again; the far side holds the first frame meanwhile.
