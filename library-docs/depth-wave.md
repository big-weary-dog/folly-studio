looks: Smooth bands of colour rolling from the front of the layer to the back, leaning up.

Uses the rig's depth, so front towers, portal and rear arches light in turn. **nave-procession** steps comets the same
way on the beat; **rising-sweep** goes up instead.

## Overrides
- bars.speed: negative rolls toward the front.
- bars.bars: waves front to back; 1.5 broad, 3–4 stripes.
- bars.direction: `front` toward the audience; `right` across.
- rotate.angle: the lean; 0 upright, −0.1 leans hard, positive the other way.

## Pairings
- Under a sparse **sparkle** layer; bind bars.speed to energy.
- Then **edge-fade** (direction `back`) to fade it toward the back.

## Pitfalls
- On a flat group (the rose, one arch) the whole group changes at once.
