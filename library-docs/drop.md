---
looks: Hits double over four bars as the light closes on the rose; two dark beats, then it slams open.
---
**bars** in body, accent and high, played as a four-bar drum part by **rhythm**: quarters, eighths, sixteenths, then
eight sixteenths and two silent beats. An **edge-fade** on `in`, its position on a saw over the 16 beats, closes the
lit area in on the rose and holds it shut for the last two beats; the downbeat throws it open, and **delay** rolls
each hit out from the middle. A dim **colour-wash** keeps the dark beats from going black. It repeats every phrase,
locked to the look's bpm.

## Overrides
- rhythm.pattern: the 64-step phrase; keep the rests at its end for the dark before the drop.
- bars.colours: the hits.
- delay.longest: how far each hit rolls out; 0 hits everywhere at once.

## Pairings
- After **stutter** or **rising-embers** as the build.
- Followed by **climbing-hits** once it has dropped.

## Pitfalls
- Rhythm's flash cap reads the whole line: one this busy only dips on every hit, so the slam is the opening, not a
  flash.
- The phrase runs on the show's clock, not the clip's: start the clip on a 16-beat line, or it drops mid-phrase.
