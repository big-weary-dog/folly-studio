Filters rework the colour of everything above them in the chain, so they go after the sources.

- Colour: recolour (from a pick, or flat Steps), hue-shift.
- Cut and fade: mask (by an input source), edge-fade.
- Soften: blur (space), trails (time).
- Time: delay (later along a direction), smoke (a trail that drifts), replay (the last moments again).
- Rearrange: sort (the bright light slides to one end of each strand).

Blur, trails, delay, smoke, replay and sort hold real LED colours, so warps below them don't reach above.
