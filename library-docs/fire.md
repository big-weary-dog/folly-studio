Noise flames rising from one edge of the layer; target the groups that should burn. **clouds** is the same noise
drifting; **bars** on `up` is a clean, steady rise.

## Params
- colours: Cooler upper flame to hottest roots, e.g. `#300000 #ff2000 #ff9000 #fff0a0`.
- tips: The flame tips and rising embers; default `high`. Set a warm pick for golden sparks.
- speed: 0.6–1.5 a hearth; 3–4 a blaze.
- height: 0.3–0.5 reads as flames; 1–1.5 fills the layer.
- embers: How many sparks rise, 0–1; they reach up to 2.5 × height, capped at the layer's top.
- size: 0.2–0.4 distinct tongues; above 1 one slow sheet.

## Pairings
- Over a dim warm **colour-wash** so the space above glows.
- Then **mirror** height: flames from top and bottom meeting.
- Then **rotate** to point the rising flames sideways or down.
- Bind speed and height to `energy`.

## Pitfalls
- Flames start from the whole layer's edge: on a whole-rig layer the spires stay dark unless height is near 1.5 or
  the layer targets them.
- Small size on the spires reads as speckle.
