---
looks: Shells bursting into spoked rings of sparks that spread, sag and fade, each one colour.
---
Celebration, finales, New Year. **ripple** for gentle rings without sparks, **shockwave** for one front from a fixed
point, **sparkle** for glitter that stays put.

## Params
- speed: 1+ is a barrage.
- shells: independent recurring burst streams, not a count of simultaneous bursts.

## Pairings
- Over a dark **colour-wash** bed; then **trails** for lingering sparks.

## Pitfalls
- Bursts sit in the layer's upper half; on the whole rig that is sparse towers that break rings into dots. Best on
  the rose, the arches or a tall dense layer.
- Each shell steps through the pick, so a short pick repeats.
- Many shells at high speed on a small layer flickers; drop shells as speed rises.
