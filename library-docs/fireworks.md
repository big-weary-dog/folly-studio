Shells burst high in the layer: a bright flash at the heart, then a ring of spark spokes spreading fast and slowing,
sagging as it falls and fading out, each burst one colour from the pick. Several shells fire independently, each at
its own place every time. Celebration, finales, New Year. Reach for **ripple** for gentle rings without sparks,
**shockwave** for one front bursting from a fixed point, **sparkle** for glitter that stays put.

## Params
- colours: `all` or `rainbow` for a mixed display, `high` for white.
- speed: Bursts a second from each shell. 0.3–0.5 lets each burst bloom and fade; 1+ is a barrage.
- shells: 1–2 for single bursts, 4–6 for a finale.
- size: A share of the layer; 0.2–0.4 suits most. 0.6 fills the layer with one burst.
- sparks: 8–16 reads as sparks; 24+ merges into a solid ring on sparse LEDs.

## Pairings
- Blend `over` a dark `colour-wash` bed (a night-sky hex) so the bursts stand out.
- Then `trails` for spark trails that linger after each burst.
- With `sparkle` in `high` above for crackle; bind speed to `energy` for a finale.

## Pitfalls
- Bursts sit in the upper half of the layer; on the whole rig that is mostly towers and spires, where sparse strands
  break rings into dots. Best on the rose, the arches or a tall dense layer.
- A burst cuts out at about 15% brightness when its shell fires again; at high speed the cut shows.
- Many shells at high speed on a small layer is a flickering mess; drop shells as speed rises.
