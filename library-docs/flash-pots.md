A **strobe** in fire colours, kept to the lowest part of the rig by **edge-fade**, whose flashes rise as **smoke**
plumes. The layer adds over what is below, so put it above the main look. It fires once a bar at the look's bpm.

## Overrides
- strobe.speed: hits in beats per (0.25 once a bar, 0.5 every two beats).
- strobe.scatter: how many pots fire at once.
- smoke.fade: plume height; lower rises higher.
- smoke.drift: how fast the plumes rise.
- strobe.colours: the flash colours.

## Pairings
- Over **willow**, **brushfire** or any bed, for a chorus's downbeats.

## Pitfalls
- Faster than every two beats, or edge-fade higher, and the flash check may refuse it.
