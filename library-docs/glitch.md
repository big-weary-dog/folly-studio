looks: Strands switching on and off at random, each in its own colour: a flicker or a glitch.
---
**strobe** with Scatter blanking a random share of strands each step and Mixed colours giving each lit strand its own
colour from the pick, so the rig flickers in a random patchwork. Reach for it for glitches, static and broken-signal
moments. **sparkle** glints single LEDs instead of whole strands; **strobe** alone flashes everything together.

## Overrides
- strobe.speed: steps a second; 2 a restless flicker, 6 a frantic glitch (the bake checks for flashing).
- strobe.scatter: the share of strands dark each step; 0.3 most lit, 0.9 a sparse few.
- strobe.length: how long each strand stays lit within its step; 0.5 half.
- strobe.fade: softens each switch off.
- strobe.mixed: off lights every strand in the step's one colour.

## Pairings
- Over a dim **colour-wash** bed, or on the spirelets alone for a small-scale flicker.

## Pitfalls
- At high speed on a large area it fails the flash check; keep it under 3 a second on the rose and the arches.
