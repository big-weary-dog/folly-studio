---
looks: Strands switching on and off at random, each in its own colour: a flicker or a glitch.
---
For glitches, static and broken-signal moments. **sparkle** glints single LEDs instead; **strobe** alone flashes
everything together.

## Overrides
- strobe.speed: steps a second; 2 restless, 6 frantic.
- strobe.scatter: share of strands dark each step; 0.3 mostly lit, 0.9 a sparse few.
- strobe.length: share of each step a strand stays lit.
- strobe.fade: softens each switch-off.
- strobe.mixed: off lights all strands in one colour per step.

## Pitfalls
- Over 3 steps a second on the rose or arches fails the flash check.
