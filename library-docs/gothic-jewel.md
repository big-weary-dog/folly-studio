A still cathedral window in jewel colours: **stained-glass** on the `spiral` rule, two petals a colour, with lead
lines, each pane breathing gently in its own time. Reach for it for a sacred, solemn or gothic idea, a calm opener,
or a bed under a slow element on the rest of the Folly. **cathedral-sun** sweeps light across instead;
**backlit-glow** lets clouds move behind the glass.

## Overrides
- stained-glass.rule: `petal` or `random` for another window; stained-glass.colours: any five colours.
- stained-glass.motion: `none` for a still window, `sweep` for sunlight; stained-glass.speed: the glow's pace.
- stained-glass.leading: 0 for seamless glass.

## Pairings
- On the `rose`; a dim **colour-wash** of the same colours on the rest of the Folly.

## Pitfalls
- On a layer wider than the rose, the glass falls back to angle and distance and paints every group: target the rose.
