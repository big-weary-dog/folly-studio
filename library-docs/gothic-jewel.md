Sacred, solemn or gothic ideas, a calm opener. **cathedral-sun** sweeps light across; **backlit-glow** moves clouds
behind the glass.

## Overrides
- stained-glass.rule: `petal` or `random` for another window.
- stained-glass.motion: `none` still, `sweep` sunlight.
- stained-glass.leading: 0 for seamless glass.

## Pairings
- A dim **colour-wash** of the same colours on the rest of the Folly.

## Pitfalls
- Target the `rose`; on a wider layer the glass paints every group by angle and distance.
