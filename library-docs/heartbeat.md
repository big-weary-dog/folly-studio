A strong swell and a softer echo (or one thump), by default once every two beats of the look's bpm, clear between.
Tempo `own` gives it its own rate. For tension, love themes and music-synced pulsing. **strobe** flashes hard;
**shockwave** bursts on its own clock.

## Params
- direction: With **anchor**, `out` starts from the rose.
- spread: 0.2–0.4 a visible swell.
- tempo: `own` with rate 50–80 and every `one` is a resting heart; rate binds like a speed.
- every: `two` calm, `one` excited, `half` at 120 bpm is a flash risk.
- shape: `single` reads as a beat rather than a heart.
- sharpness: 0.5 a natural swell, 0.8+ a knock, near 0 a breath.
- second: 0.5–0.8 a heart; 1 two equal thumps.

## Pairings
- Over a dark **colour-wash** bed so the pause keeps a colour.
- Then **anchor** `rose`, shape `sphere`: beats through the towers from the rose.

## Pitfalls
- To slow it, lower the tempo, raise every, or use tempo `own`.
- Spread 0 above ~3 beats a second pulses the whole layer: flash risk.
