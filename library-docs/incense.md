A slow **heartbeat** of its own tempo, spreading out from the rose (**anchor**), whose light rises as curling
**smoke**, over a dim **colour-wash**. Ritual and ceremony, a held moment of reverence, a meditative interlude.

## Overrides
- heartbeat.rate: pulses a minute.
- heartbeat.spread: how long a pulse takes to spread out from the rose.
- smoke.drift: rise.
- smoke.curl: straight wisps or billowing puffs.

## Pairings
- After **daybreak**, or under **rising-embers** held low.

## Pitfalls
- heartbeat.tempo is `own`: it ignores the bpm. Set `look` to pulse with the music.
