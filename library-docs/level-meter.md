**sparkle** lights LEDs at random; **sort** piles every lit one at the strand's feet, so how many are lit becomes
how high it fills; **trails** lets it fall back softly; a dim **colour-wash** beneath. The arches read as a giant
equaliser. Bind sparkle.density to a saw LFO in beats instead and every strand kicks on the beat to its own height.

## Overrides
- sparkle.density: the level; a number fixes it.
- sparkle.often: jitter; lower is calmer.
- trails.decay: fall; higher falls slower.
- sort.direction: `down` fills from the feet, `up` from the apex.

## Pairings
- Under **climbing-hits** at a peak, energy automated up.

## Pitfalls
- Reads on upright strands (arch legs, towers); on the rose it sorts round the rings.
