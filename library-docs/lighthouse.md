On a tall layer the beams are upright lines sweeping side to side; on a flat one they turn like clock hands. Use
**spire-helix** for stripes winding the towers, **galaxy** for spiral arms.

## Overrides
- bars.direction: `orbit-counterclockwise` or `orbit-clockwise` (as seen from above).
- bars.speed: with 2 beams, 0.4 is one turn every 5 s; negative reverses.
- bars.bars: beams on the circle, 1–4.
- bars.duty: beam plus wake; 0.3 short, 0.9 long.
- bars.colours: beam colours in turn; `high` for one warm white.
- bars.twist: spirals the beams with height.

## Pairings
- Then **anchor** (tag `tower`, which `nearest`, shape `plan`): a beam round each tower.
- Then **trails**: light-painted circles.

## Pitfalls
- On a front-only group (an arch) it reads as a band sliding side to side.
- Over 3 passes a second on the whole rig is flashing.
