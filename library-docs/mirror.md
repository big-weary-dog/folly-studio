looks: The layer folded onto itself: matching halves, wedges, rings, or chases meeting mid-wire.
---
The layer's far half shows the near half reflected, so the portal is symmetrical left to right (or front to back,
or top to bottom); Wedges folds the circle round the centre into mirrored wedges, like a kaleidoscope, and Rings
repeats it out from the centre in rings, every other one mirrored; and with `along` every wire's second half mirrors its first, so a chase runs in from both ends and
meets in the middle. A warp: it bends the sources and filters above it in its layer, up to a blur or trails, so it
goes after what it folds. Reach for it whenever a look should read as designed rather than random; plasma folded
across is the preset butterfly, Wedges the preset kaleidoscope, Rings the preset tunnel. The folds hold still: put
**scroll** `round` below Wedges to turn them, `out` below Rings to move the rings outward. **polar** reshapes rather
than folds.

## Params
- across: The viewer's left half is drawn and reflected onto the right.
- depth: Makes the front of the towers match their back; does nothing on a flat layer.
- height: The lower half is drawn and repeated upside down above. Rising fire becomes flames from both ends.
- wedges: Mirrored wedges round the centre (0 off); each shows the same slice, so 8 shows 16 pieces. Their edges
  sit on the rose's petal edges, so 2, 4, 8 and 16 fit its 16 petals (16 halves each petal). Turn across off with it.
- rings: Copies from the centre to the edge (0 off), always even so the rings join with no seam, also under
  **scroll** `out`. On the rose's 7 rings more than 4 aliases; on a whole-rig layer 4–8 reads.
- along: The first half of each wire plays; the second echoes it backwards. Combines with the others.

## Pairings
- After clouds or bars on `round`: instant symmetry (preset butterfly).
- With `along` after single-strand or arch-chase: comets leave both feet of each arch and meet at the keystone.
- Wedges 8 after bars `round` swirled by **twist**, then **scroll** `round` and **anchor** `rose`: a turning
  kaleidoscope centred on the rose (preset kaleidoscope).
- Rings 4 after clouds, then **scroll** `out` and **anchor** `rose` (`sphere`): rings flowing out of the rose
  (preset tunnel).
- Put **anchor** below it to fold round a group's middle (the rose) instead of the layer's.

## Pitfalls
- Wedges read only a thin slice of what is above (just clockwise of 12 o'clock): a source that changes only across
  the layer (bars `right`) shows almost one colour. Feed it something that varies with angle and distance.
- All toggles off does nothing. A source that is already symmetrical (bars going up, a centred spot) shows no change
  with `across`.
- Motion going right on the drawn half comes back leftward on the other: a sweep becomes two that meet and part.
- The fold is round the layer's own box: a layer on one tower folds that tower, not the whole portal.
