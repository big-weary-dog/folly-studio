Folds what is above it so the look reads as designed: halves, kaleidoscope wedges, rings from the centre, or each
wire from its middle. Goes after what it folds (presets **butterfly**, **kaleidoscope**, **tunnel**). Folds hold
still: **rotate** Spin below wedges turns them; **scroll** `out` or `in` below rings moves them
out or in. **polar** reshapes rather than folds.

## Params
- wedges: 0 and 1 are off. Edges sit on the rose's petal edges, so 2, 4, 8, 16 fit. Turn across off with it.
- rings: Always even, so no seam. Fine repeats can alias on the rose, but 8–12 still changes it; try 4–8 first.

## Pairings
- `along` after **single-strand**: comets leave both feet of each arch and meet at the keystone.
- **anchor** below it folds round a group's middle (the rose) instead of the layer's.

## Pitfalls
- Wedges sample a thin slice just clockwise of 12 o'clock: feed a source that varies with angle and distance, not
  bars `right`.
- A symmetrical source shows no change with `across`.
- The fold is round the layer's own box: on one tower it folds that tower.
