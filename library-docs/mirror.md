looks: The layer folded onto itself: two matching halves, or chases meeting mid-wire.
---
The layer's far half shows the near half reflected, so the portal is symmetrical left to right (or front to back,
or top to bottom), and with `along` every wire's second half mirrors its first, so a chase runs in from both ends and
meets in the middle. A warp: it bends the sources and filters above it in its layer, up to a blur or trails, so it
goes after what it folds. Reach for it whenever a look should read as designed rather than random; plasma folded
across is the preset butterfly. **kaleidoscope** folds round a centre into many wedges instead of across a line,
**radial-repeat** repeats in rings, **polar** reshapes rather than folds.

## Params
- across: folds left ↔ right round the layer's middle; the viewer's left half is drawn and reflected onto the right.
- depth: folds front ↔ back, so the front of the towers matches their back; does nothing on a flat layer.
- height: folds up ↔ down; the lower half is drawn and repeated upside down above. Rising fire becomes flames from
  both ends.
- along: folds every strand at its middle: the first half of each wire plays, the second half echoes it backwards.
  Combines with the others.

## Pairings
- After plasma, clouds or bars on `round`: instant symmetry (preset butterfly).
- With `along` after single-strand or arch-chase: comets leave both feet of each arch and meet at the keystone.
- Put **anchor** below it to fold round a group's middle (the rose) instead of the layer's.

## Pitfalls
- All toggles off does nothing. A source that is already symmetrical (bars going up, a centred spot) shows no change
  with `across`.
- Motion going right on the drawn half comes back leftward on the other: a sweep becomes two that meet and part.
- The fold is round the layer's own box: a layer on one tower folds that tower, not the whole portal.
