looks: Each strand broken into flat runs of LEDs, one colour a run: a chunky, 8-bit look.

Each run takes the colour at its middle, so smooth sources step down the wire. Place it after the source.
**recolour** with Steps flattens colours instead; **mirror** folds.

## Pairings
- After smooth **clouds** or **bars**; then **recolour** Steps for pixel art.

## Pitfalls
- No effect on the rose (one-LED panes).
- Long runs on a fast source flash many LEDs at once; keep it slow (3 Hz flash check).
