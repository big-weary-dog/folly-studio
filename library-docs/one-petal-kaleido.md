A mandala on the rose: clouds drift through one petal, and petal-repeat copies it round all sixteen, alternate
petals mirrored; the rest of the layer stays plain clouds. **kaleidoscope** turns mirrored wedges over the whole
layer instead.

## Overrides
- petal-repeat.petal: picks the motif; the other petals of the same clouds look quite different.
- petal-repeat.repeats: `eight` or `four` keep more of the source, less symmetry.
- clouds.speed: how fast the motif changes.

## Pitfalls
- A petal the clouds barely cover goes nearly dark everywhere on the rose: change petal or raise clouds.cover.
