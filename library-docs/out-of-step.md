looks: Five blocks thump together, drift into a canon, land back in step.
---
A **heartbeat** on the look's tempo, then **delay** in four steps across the facade, its longest delay swept by a
slow triangle LFO (one cycle every 32 beats). At 120 bpm the blocks drift a whole beat apart at the top and land back
in step, like Steve Reich's Piano Phase. Over a dim **colour-wash**.

## Overrides
- delay.longest: how far apart; set its LFO's top to 240 ÷ bpm seconds to realign at other tempos.
- delay.steps: blocks less one.
- delay.direction: `right`, `up`, or `out` from the rose.
- heartbeat.sharpness: thump.
- heartbeat.every: thumps every beat or two.

## Pairings
- With **call-and-answer** for a rhythmic section.

## Pitfalls
- It thumps every two beats: every beat, the blocks out of step flash too often for the flash check.
