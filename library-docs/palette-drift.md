The calmest full-coverage bed, for openings, ambient stretches and under sparse elements. **butterfly** is busier and
symmetrical; plain **colour-wash** has no gradient.

## Overrides
- bars.colours: a role (`body`) stays in the palette; `rainbow` ignores it.
- bars.speed: 0.05 near-still, 0.3 visible change; negative sinks.
- bars.brightness: 0.2–0.4 to sit under an element.
- bars.bars: fewer gives a broad gradient, more packs in bands.
- bars.direction: `out` or `in` rings, `clockwise` or `counterclockwise` a colour wheel.
- turbulence.amount: 0 straight bands, 0.3 marbling.

## Pairings
- With **sparkle** in `high` over it for a glittering calm.
- Bind bars.brightness to energy (`from` 0.2 `to` 1).

## Pitfalls
- A single colour pick gives no visible bands; use several colours.
