The calmest full-coverage bed, for openings, ambient stretches and under sparse elements. **butterfly** is busier and
symmetrical; plain **colour-wash** has no gradient.

## Overrides
- colour-wash.colours: a role (`body`) stays in the palette; `rainbow` ignores it.
- colour-wash.speed: 0.05 near-still, 0.3 visible change.
- colour-wash.brightness: 0.2–0.4 to sit under an element.
- hue-shift.spread: 0 one colour; 0.5–1 a full rainbow up the layer.
- hue-shift.direction: `out` or `in` rings from or to the centre, `clockwise` or `counterclockwise` a colour
  wheel.
- hue-shift.speed: makes the gradient climb (0.05 slow; negative sinks).
- turbulence.amount: 0 straight bands, 0.3 marbling.

## Pairings
- With **sparkle** in `high` over it for a glittering calm.
- Bind colour-wash.brightness to energy (`from` 0.2 `to` 1).

## Pitfalls
- Large spread leaves the palette; whites and greys barely change.
