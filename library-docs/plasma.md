Smooth **bars** of every colour in the pick, many across the layer and drifting sideways, pushed around by
**turbulence** into a moving, marbled field that covers every LED. Reach for it for trippy, busy beds and 70s/90s
energy. **clouds** is the calm, soft relative with clear gaps; **palette-drift** is a gentler drifting field;
**butterfly** is this mirrored.

## Overrides
- bars.bars: how many colour bands across the layer; 6 gives broad blobs, 16 busy marbling. Enough to show the
  whole pick, or only part of the palette is ever on at once.
- bars.speed: how fast the colours drift through; 0.1 lazy, 0.6 churning.
- bars.colours: two or three contrasting colours read best; a long pick turns to mush.
- bars.direction: `up` or `in` (round the rose, with **anchor**) drift the field another way.
- turbulence.amount: how far the bands are bent; 0.2 gentle waves, 0.5 full swirl.
- turbulence.size: a swirl's size; 0.15 fine curls, 0.5 broad bends.

## Pairings
- Add **mirror** (across) for symmetrical wings (**butterfly**), or **mirror** Wedges round the rose for a mandala.
- Add **recolour** with Steps for flat poster bands of colour.

## Pitfalls
- It covers the whole layer opaquely, so a bed under it is wasted; it is the bed.
- Turbulence size below ~0.15 on the sparse spires and towers turns to speckle.
