A busy, opaque bed with 70s/90s energy. **clouds** is calmer with gaps; **palette-drift** gentler; **butterfly** is
this mirrored.

## Overrides
- bars.bars: 6 broad blobs, 16 busy marbling; too few shows only part of the pick.
- bars.speed: 0.1 lazy, 0.6 churning.
- bars.colours: two or three contrasting colours; a long pick turns to mush.
- turbulence.amount: 0.2 gentle waves, 0.5 full swirl.
- turbulence.size: 0.15 fine curls, 0.5 broad; smaller speckles on spires and towers.

## Pairings
- Then **recolour** with Steps for flat poster bands.

## Pitfalls
- Opaque over the whole layer: a bed under it is wasted.
