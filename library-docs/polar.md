Wraps what is above it round the centre: across becomes angle (clockwise from 12), up becomes distance. Bars `right`
become spokes, bars `up` rings, fire a sun. **mirror** Wedges folds instead; **twist** after it makes spirals.

## Pairings
- Then **anchor** as the last warp to centre on the rose.

## Pitfalls
- A seam at 12 o'clock unless the source tiles across (whole numbers of bars).
- Distance runs to the farthest corner, so on the portal much of the source lands off the arches; anchor with a reach.
- Façade only; depth is ignored.
