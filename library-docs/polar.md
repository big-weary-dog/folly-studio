What is above it is wrapped round the layer's centre: its across (left to right) becomes the angle round the centre,
clockwise from 12 o'clock, and its up becomes the distance out. So bars going right become turning spokes, bars going
up become rings moving out, and a fire becomes a sun. A warp: it bends the modules above it in its layer up to a blur
or trails, so it goes after the source. Reach for it to make radial looks from any linear source; **anchor** below it
moves the centre to the rose, **twist** after it curls spokes into spirals (preset galaxy), **kaleidoscope** mirrors
wedges instead.

## Params
- repeats: 2–4 makes several spokes from one bar.
- inward: On, rising sources fall inward.

## Pairings
- Bars `right` then polar then twist (swirl ≈ −1): spiral arms (preset galaxy); a spot over the centre for a core.
- Fire or climb then polar: flames or bars radiating from the centre like a sunburst.
- Then **anchor** (rose) as the last warp: the circle centred on the rose window.

## Pitfalls
- A visible seam at 12 o'clock where the source's left and right edges meet, unless the source tiles across (whole
  numbers of bars, `round`-friendly sources).
- Distance runs to the layer's farthest corner, so on the wide portal most of the source's top lands off the arches;
  anchor with a reach to control it.
- Works in the façade; towers' depth is ignored.
