Presets are tuned chains, usually a bed and an element. Use one as `{ "preset": "<name>" }` when it is the idea, with
`overrides` for the params its doc lists, or copy its recipe into `modules` to change more. Palette roles follow the
look's palette.
A preset that rides one fader brings it as a macro the look gains (`dawn`, `stutter`, `build`); automate it, or
the preset holds still at the macro's value. Some are meant as a layer over a look (`flash-pots` adds its hits).
