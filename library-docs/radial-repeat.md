looks: The layer repeated in rings round its centre, flowing outward like ripples.
---
The whole layer, from its centre out to its farthest corner, is squeezed into each of Rings concentric rings, and the
rings drift outward (or inward) at Speed. A warp: it bends the modules above it in its layer up to a blur or trails,
so it goes after the source. With Mirror every other ring is reflected, so there is no hard seam where one ring meets
the next. Reach for it to turn any texture into a target or ripple; **bars** on `in`/`out` makes plainer rings
directly, **kaleidoscope** repeats round the angle instead, **scroll** `out` slides without repeating.

## Params
- rings: 1–2 keeps the source readable; 4–8 is a target. Each ring shows the source's full centre-to-corner span, so
  more rings means more squeezed detail.
- speed: Rings a second; 0.1–0.3 for a calm pulse.
- mirror: On gives a smooth, breathing in-out look; off shows a sharp edge where the source's rim meets its centre.

## Pairings
- After clouds or fire: textured ripples. After a still colour-wash nothing changes; give it a source that
  varies with distance.
- Put **anchor** (rose, `sphere` reach) below it: ripples from the rose over the whole portal.
- Edge-fade on `out` after it fades the outer rings away.

## Pitfalls
- Off mirror plus fast speed on a high-contrast source makes the seam sweep the whole rig; above ~2 rings a second it
  reads as flashing.
- Works in the façade: towers' depth is ignored.
- The centre is the layer's own; on a whole-rig layer that is mid-portal, not the rose, unless anchored.
