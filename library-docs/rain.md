Streaks with bright `high` heads and `accent` tails fall down the layer in 30 lanes and six depth slots over a dim
`low` bed, each drop at its own pace; at low energy a third of the lanes run, at full energy all of them: **streaks**
over a **colour-wash**. Steady weather or melancholy. **snowstorm** slants and swirls; **snowflakes** drifts slow
dots; **meteors** follows each wire rather than falling straight down.

## Overrides
- streaks.speed: layers a second; 0.9 brisk, 2 a downpour, 0.3 drizzle.
- streaks.density: share of lanes in use, bound to energy 0.3→1; a number fixes how heavy.
- streaks.tail: streak length as a share of the layer; 0.05 drops, 0.3 long rods.
- streaks.lanes: how many columns across; fewer for sparser fat streaks.
- streaks.angle: slant in turns off straight down; 0.05 a breeze.
- streaks.depth: 1 for a flat layer; more varies drops front to back.
- streaks.colours: tail to head; colour-wash.colours and colour-wash.brightness: the bed.

## Pairings
- With **storm** on a layer beneath; automate energy upward to build the rain.
- Over **water** for rain on a sea.

## Pitfalls
- A lane with no LEDs in it shows no rain: on sparse groups raise lanes or density to fill them.
- On wires running sideways (arches' crowns) drops cross in a blink; it reads best on the towers and arch legs.
