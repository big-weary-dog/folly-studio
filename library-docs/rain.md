**streaks** over a dim **colour-wash**; a third of the lanes run at low energy, all at full. Steady weather or
melancholy. **snowstorm** slants and swirls; **meteors** follows each wire.

## Overrides
- streaks.speed: 0.3 drizzle, 0.9 brisk, 2 a downpour.
- streaks.density: a number fixes how heavy.
- streaks.angle: 0.05 a breeze.
- streaks.depth: 1 for a flat layer.

## Pairings
- **storm** on a layer beneath; automate energy upward to build.
- Over **water** for rain on a sea.

## Pitfalls
- Lanes with no LEDs show no rain: on sparse groups raise lanes or density.
- Reads best on towers and arch legs; on sideways wires drops cross in a blink.
