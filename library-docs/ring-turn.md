looks: The rose window's rings stepped round by whole petals, each further than the last: a stepped spiral.

Turns each ring of the rose by whole petals, the heart still and each ring a step further out, so broad bands on the
rose become spiral arms. Goes after the source; only the rose moves. **twist** swirls smoothly instead of by whole
petals; **scroll** `petal` turns the whole rose at once.

## Params
- step: 1 or −1 a gentle spiral; 2–3 tight arms; with alternate on, the rings interlock.
  8 is a half-turn; saved −8 becomes the equivalent +8.
- speed: about 0.25 steps every few seconds; above 1 reads as flicker, not a turn.

## Pairings
- After **bars** `right` (smooth, 4–6 bars) or medium **clouds**: their bands become spiral arms.

## Pitfalls
- A source that changes only with distance from the heart (bars `out` or `in`, mirror rings) shows no change.
- A source the same in every petal (a chase on every strand alike) shows no change either.
