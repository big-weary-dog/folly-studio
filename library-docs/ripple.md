Rings from random drops, the newer cutting the older. Calm and watery on any broad surface. **rose-ripple** is steady
rings from the rose; **shockwave** one burst.

## Overrides
- in-turn.drops: 1–2 occasional, 5–8 steady rain.
- in-turn.every: seconds between drops; a drop lives drops × every. Set bars.speed to −1 ÷ that.
- in-turn.reach: ring spread, a share of the layer's longer side.
- bars.duty: 0.05 fine circles, 0.3 soft blooms.

## Pairings
- Over a dim **colour-wash**; **blur** on sparse groups keeps rings whole.

## Pitfalls
- Façade plane only: a single tower shows arcs and slices.
