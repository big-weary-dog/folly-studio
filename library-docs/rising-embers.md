---
**sparkle** embers whose **smoke** rises up each strand, revealed from the ground by **edge-fade**, over a dim
**colour-wash**. Density, rise and height follow the preset's `build` macro. Ride it through a riser and pull it down
at the drop.

## Overrides
- sparkle.colours: ember colour.
- smoke.spread: plume width.
- smoke.fade: how long each ember trails.

## Pairings
- Over **bonfire** for sparks above the fire.
- Before **climbing-hits** at the top of a build.

## Pitfalls
- sparkle.density and often, smoke.drift and edge-fade.position follow `build`: automate it, or set it on the
  look's fader.
