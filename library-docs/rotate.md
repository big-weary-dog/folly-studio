looks: The layer tilted by an angle or turning steadily: in the façade, from the side or in plan.

Sets a linear source at a slant or turning. A warp: it goes after the source and stops at a blur or trails. **twist**
turns more at the rim, **scroll** slides, **mirror** with Wedges mirrors round the centre.

## Params
- plane: `side` leans front-to-back motion; `plan` only shows on layers with depth.
- angle: The default 0.125 (45°) tilts at once; set 0 for spin only. ±0.02–0.05 is a subtle lean.
- spin: 0.02–0.1 slow; above ~0.5 big stripes flicker.

## Pairings
- Bars going `back`, then `side` with a small angle: waves rolling front to back.
- Bars, then `facade` with spin: a turning fan; **mirror** below makes it symmetrical.
- **anchor** below it turns round a group's middle.

## Pitfalls
- Corners turn outside the source's box, where some sources repeat, clamp or go dark.
