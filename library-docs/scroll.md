Slides what is above it and wraps it round; goes after the source. Sets a still source (frozen clouds, a gradient, a
mosaic) in motion; `petal` and `ring` step a whole rose petal or ring at a time. **rotate** turns,
**turbulence** pushes unevenly.

## Params
- direction: `up`, `down`, `right`, `left`, `back`, `front`, `out`, `in`, `along`, `petal` or `ring`.
  For continuous turning, use **rotate** Spin.
- speed: 0.02–0.2 is a drift.

## Pairings
- With `along` after a still **sparkle**: lights creep up every strand.
- With `petal` under **stained-glass** on the rose: the glass ticks round a pane at a time.

## Pitfalls
- Wrapping shows a seam unless the source tiles (bars with whole numbers).
- Above ~1 on a high-contrast source can fail the flash check.
- Speeds add with a source moving the same way; opposite ways cancel.
