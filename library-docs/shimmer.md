A textured bed alive without moving: sequins, frost, heat haze. **starfield** is sparse glints on a dark sky;
**sparkle** in pulse mode gives discrete twinkles.

## Overrides
- sparkle.rate: 8 shimmers, 1–2 slow twinkle, over 12 static.
- sparkle.sharp: 3 lively, 1–2 floods with the high colour, 6–8 the odd spark.
- sparkle.density: 0.3 leaves most of the bed still.
- bars.speed: the bed's climb; 0 holds it.

## Pairings
- Bind sparkle.rate or sparkle.density to energy.
- Then **mask** with **clouds** input to glitter in drifting patches.

## Pitfalls
- Keep sharp ≥ 3 when rate is over 6, or the rig flickers.
