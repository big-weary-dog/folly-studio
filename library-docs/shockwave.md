A repeating ring burst from the rose in 3D, bright at its front. For impacts, drops and booms. **ripple** is many rings
from random points; **heartbeat** swells to the tempo.

## Overrides
- bars.speed: bursts a second, negative heads out; for one a beat, −bpm ÷ 60.
- bars.duty: 0.15 crisp, 0.6 broad.
- edge-fade.width: 1 fades out fully, 0.3 stays bright to the edge.
- anchor.tag: where it bursts from.

## Pitfalls
- Steady speed, no easing; a saw LFO on bars.speed comes close.
- Not beat-locked unless speed is set from the tempo.
