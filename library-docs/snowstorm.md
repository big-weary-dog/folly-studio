A blizzard, wind, a cold build. **rain** falls straight; **snowflakes** drifts slow dots and settles.

## Overrides
- streaks.angle: turns off straight down; ±0.25 sideways.
- streaks.speed: 0.6 gusty, 1.5 a gale.
- streaks.density: bound to energy 0.35→1; a number fixes how heavy.
- streaks.lanes: fewer, bigger flakes on sparse groups; flakes use four lanes each.
- turbulence.amount: 0.15 eddies.

## Pairings
- Automate energy 0.3→1 over a section; crossfade from **snowflakes** for calm before the storm.

## Pitfalls
- Fine flakes can miss sparse LEDs; lower lanes to make them bigger.
- Turbulence over ~0.2 breaks the streaks into noise.
