Sources draw: each puts one element (bands, flames, sparks, a bolt) on transparent, coloured from its one `colours`
pick, and fills the layer it lands on. A chain's first source covers; later ones add unless given a blend. Choose by
the motion the idea names: a whole-layer change (colour-wash, clouds), something that sweeps (bars),
something that runs along the wires (single-strand, life), particles (sparkle, streaks, fireworks, lightning) or a
beat (strobe, heartbeat). The in-turn warp plays any of them once per group or drop, in turn. Many looks are presets
of these: plasma, glow, shockwave, glitch, curtain-reveal, lighthouse, ripple, arch-chase.
