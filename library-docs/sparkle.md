looks: Single LEDs glinting at random, each in its own colour: stars, glitter, sequins.

The cheapest texture and the usual top layer: pulse for twinkling stars, smooth for sequins. **life** gives structured
flicker along strands; **streaks** moves.

## Params
- colours: `high` gives white stars.
- density: 0.1–0.3 stars, 1 all-over shimmer.
- rate: Each LED ±50%. 0.2–0.6 stars, 4–10 glitter in smooth.
- length: 1 never goes dark: slow breathing.

## Pairings
- Last, blend `over` or `add` on any bed.
- With **clouds** as input to **mask**: glints in patches.

## Pitfalls
- Pulse at rate over 2 with high density reads as static.
- Raise density on small, sparse groups or they go nearly dark.
