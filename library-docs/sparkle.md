The cheapest texture and the usual top layer: a short flash twinkles like stars, a long one glows like sequins.
**life** gives structured flicker along strands; **streaks** moves.

## Params
- colours: `high` gives white stars.
- density: 0.1–0.3 stars, 1 all-over shimmer.
- often: Each LED ±50%. 12–40 stars, 240–600 glitter with a glowing flash.
- flash: 0.1–0.25 a glint; 0.5 never goes dark: slow breathing; 0.6+ glows between levels.

## Pairings
- Last, blend `over` or `add` on any bed.
- With **clouds** as input to **mask**: glints in patches.

## Pitfalls
- A short flash at often over 120 with high density reads as static.
- Raise density on small, sparse groups or they go nearly dark.
