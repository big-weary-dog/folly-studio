looks: The rose as a cathedral window: glass panes by petal, ring or spiral, lead lines, backlit.
---
Lit by backlight plus the brightness of what is above it, so after a source (**clouds**, **bars**) the light moves
while the colours stay. **picture** draws a shape instead; **recolour** keeps the shapes above.

## Params
- colours: jewel tones `#9b111e #0f52ba #1f8a4c #ffb000 #6a2c9c` read as a cathedral window; dim colours make dull
  glass.
- backlight: 0.3–0.5 under a moving source.
- speed: faster than 0.2 reads as flicker.

## Pairings
- **heartbeat** above with backlight 0.3: the window pulses.
- A dim **colour-wash** on the rest of the Folly.

## Pitfalls
- Target the `rose`; elsewhere it falls back to angle and distance, like a pie chart.
