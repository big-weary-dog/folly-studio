Quiet night sky for intros and night scenes. **shimmer** glitters continuously; **meteors** adds motion.

## Overrides
- sparkle.density: 0.15 sparse, 0.8 dense.
- sparkle.often: 21 calm, 90 busy.
- sparkle.flash: 0.1 pinpricks, 0.3 slow swells.
- clouds.cover: how much of the sky the multiplying clouds tint; 0 leaves only thin wisps.
- clouds.colours: the tint, multiplied into the sky. colour-wash.colours: the sky.

## Pitfalls
- Single-LED fixtures (spirelets) wink as whole lamps; lower density or leave them out.
