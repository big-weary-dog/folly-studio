A **picture** of 12 frames lighting one ring at a time, heart to rim and back, each ring a palette colour, stepped
twice a beat; on the whole rig the arches take the outer rings by distance. **trails** lets each note ring; a dim
**colour-wash** beneath.

## Overrides
- picture.speed: rate in beats per (1 quarters, 2 eighths).
- trails.decay: ringing; 0.1 staccato blips, 0.8 a harp.
- colour-wash.brightness: the bed.

## Pairings
- With **incense** for a quiet interlude, or over **out-of-step**.

## Pitfalls
- picture.speed is bound to the beat; a number makes it frames a second.
