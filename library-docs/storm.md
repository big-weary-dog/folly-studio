Drama, tension, a breakdown: from one strike every 5 s at low energy to one every 1.5 s at high. **rain** and
**snowstorm** are steady weather; **strobe** is rhythmic.

## Overrides
- lightning.speed: strikes a second; a number fixes the rate.
- lightning.width, lightning.jag and lightning.glow: bolt thickness, zigzag, halo.
- rotate.angle: 0 strikes down; 0.5 from the ground; ±0.25 sideways. Rotate follows Lightning in the chain.
- lightning.colours: glow to core.
- clouds.cover, clouds.speed and clouds.size: amount, roll speed, scale.
- mask.amount: 1 makes clear sky transparent; lower leaves a dim ghost of the wash.
- colour-wash.colours and colour-wash.brightness: cloud colour and dimness.

## Pairings
- Under a **rain** layer, with energy building.

## Pitfalls
- A bolt is one zigzag across the layer, so on the whole rig most miss the towers; use a layer per tower.
- Don't stack storm layers for more strikes; it would flash.
- A dark palette thins the clouds.
