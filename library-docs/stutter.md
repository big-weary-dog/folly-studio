Comets (**single-strand**) over a dim **colour-wash**, then **replay** loops the last beats; a second layer adds
**strobe** hits on every beat. Everything follows the preset's `stutter` macro: 0 is live motion, 1 tight half-beat
loops with hits. A whole rising-tension section on one fader; pull it down at the drop.

## Overrides
- replay.play: `loop`, `bounce` for a rocking roll, `reverse`.
- single-strand.colours: the comets.
- single-strand.speed: how fast they race.
- strobe.colours: the hits.

## Pairings
- Before **climbing-hits** or a bright drop.

## Pitfalls
- replay.length, hold and amount and the hits' brightness are bound to `stutter`; a number there leaves the fader.
- Tight loops repeat four times a second on small areas: the bake check may warn.
