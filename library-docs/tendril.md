A few tendrils wander the layer on smooth random paths, each trailing a body that tapers and darkens behind it: the
last colour at the head, the first at the tail. Organic, lazy and hypnotic; vines, eels, wisps of smoke. Paths cover
the façade plane and often hug the layer's edges. Reach for **circles** for blobs that bounce and merge,
**single-strand** comets for motion locked to each wire, **streaks** for many straight trails.

## Params
- colours: A smooth ramp like `"low accent high"` reads best.
- speed: 0.1–0.3 is a slow drift; 0.6+ darts about.
- tendrils: 1–3 on small groups, 4–6 on the whole rig.
- length: 0.2–0.4; at 1 the body breaks into beads.
- width: A share of the layer; the tail tapers to 30% of it. 0.05–0.1; thinner breaks up on sparse LEDs.

## Pairings
- Blend `over` a dim `colour-wash` or slow `plasma` bed.
- Then `mirror` across for pairs dancing in symmetry, or `kaleidoscope` on the rose for a turning flower.
- Then `trails` for a longer, smoky afterglow.

## Pitfalls
- Paths are stretched past the layer and clamped, so tendrils spend time pinned along the edges and corners.
- Long length with thin width leaves gaps between the body's beads.
- The heaviest source here per LED (every LED checks every tendril's 20 body points); avoid stacking several.
