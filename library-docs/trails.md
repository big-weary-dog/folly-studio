---
looks: A fading afterglow behind everything that moves: comet tails, light smears.
---
Each LED shows the stronger of now and its fading afterglow, by transparency, so moving sparks, bars and bolts leave
tails. **blur** smears in space instead; **bars** `comet` and **single-strand** draw their own tails.

## Params
- decay: 0.5–0.8 for long, ghostly smears.

## Pitfalls
- It ends a stage: warps below it don't bend what is above; put warps above it.
- A bed above it leaves no trail; put beds below with blend `under`.
- Seeking back or jumping over a second clears it.
