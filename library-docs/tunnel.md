Textured rings flying out from the rose through the whole rig. **rose-ripple** spreads plain rings; **kaleidoscope**
folds into wedges.

## Overrides
- mirror.rings: even; 2 broad, 8 tight.
- scroll.speed: 0.05 drifts, 0.2 rushes; negative flies inward.
- clouds.cover and clouds.size: fullness and fineness of the texture.
- clouds.colours: ring colours; colour-wash.colours and colour-wash.brightness the bed.
- anchor.tag: the group the rings come from.

## Pairings
- With a **glow** on the rose above it: a light at the end of the tunnel.

## Pitfalls
- On the rose alone over 4 rings aliases.
- Keep scroll.speed under 0.3 or it flickers.
