looks: The layer pushed around by slow noise: wobbling, rolling, heat-haze edges.
---
What is above it is displaced up/down and across by smoothly changing noise, so straight edges ripple, bands roll
like a water surface and stripes wobble like heat haze. A warp: it bends the modules above it in its layer up to a
blur or trails, so it goes after the source. Reach for it to make anything geometric feel organic: a still bar with a
little turbulence is a rolling water surface (preset water), streaks with turbulence swirl like snow (preset
snowstorm), a rainbow wash drifts (preset palette-drift). **twist** swirls in an orderly way round a centre,
**scroll** slides evenly, **clouds** and **plasma** are noise as a source rather than a push.

## Params
- amount: 0.03–0.1 ripples edges; the default 0.15 bends wide bars; 0.2+ tears shapes apart.
- size: A share of the layer. 0.1–0.2 is fine jitter that sparse LEDs cannot show; 0.3–0.7 reads well on the rig.
- speed: 0.05–0.3 is calm; above 1 it boils.

## Pairings
- After bars (one bar, duty 0.5, speed 0): a wavy water line (preset water).
- After streaks or sparkle: particles that drift and swirl (preset snowstorm).
- After hue-shift on a colour-wash: hues that drift unevenly (preset palette-drift).

## Pitfalls
- It pushes only up/down and across the façade, never front to back.
- On a flat colour it does nothing visible: it needs edges or gradients to move.
- Large amount with fast speed on a high-contrast source swaps colour on big areas quickly; keep speed modest.
