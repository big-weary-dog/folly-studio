looks: The layer pushed around by slow noise: wobbling, rolling, heat-haze edges.
---
Pushes what is above it around with smooth noise, so edges ripple and bands roll; goes after the source. Makes
geometric looks organic (presets **water**, **snowstorm**, **palette-drift**). **twist** swirls round a centre,
**scroll** slides evenly, **clouds** is noise as a source.

## Params
- amount: 0.03–0.1 ripples edges; 0.2+ tears shapes apart.
- size: 0.1–0.2 is too fine for sparse LEDs; 0.3–0.7 reads.
- speed: 0.05–0.3 calm; above 1 boils.

## Pitfalls
- Never pushes front to back.
- A flat colour shows nothing: it needs edges or gradients.
- Large amount, fast speed and high contrast flash big areas.
