What is above it is turned round the layer's centre more toward the rim than at the middle, so straight spokes and
stripes curl into a spiral, and the whole swirl turns at Spin. Wind separately turns depth round height, so on the
towers and spires a stripe winds round as it climbs. A warp: it bends the modules above it in its layer up to a blur
or trails, so it goes after the source; after **polar** it curls spokes into spiral arms (preset galaxy). **rotate**
turns everything by the same angle, **kaleidoscope** mirrors wedges, **turbulence** swirls at random.

## Params
- swirl: In turns at the rim (none at the centre). ±0.25 is a gentle curl, ±1 a tight spiral.
- wind: 1 winds a stripe once round each tower. Needs a layer with depth.
- spin: Turns a second; the default 0.05 turns slowly.

## Pairings
- Bars `right` → polar → twist (swirl −1.2, spin 0) → spot over the centre: a galaxy.
- Bars `right` or lighthouse on a tower layer, then twist with wind 1–3: barber-pole helices.
- Then **anchor** (rose) as the last warp: a whirlpool in the rose window.

## Pitfalls
- Spin defaults to 0.05: adding twist also sets the layer turning. Set spin 0 for a still swirl.
- Wind on a flat layer (the arches alone) does not spiral; it squeezes and flips the pattern across with height.
- A large swirl on sparse LEDs near the rim breaks fine detail into speckle; keep the source's features big.
