---
looks: Water on the wiring: drops send ripples that cross, add, cancel and bounce off strand ends.
---
Each LED is a little water, pulled toward its neighbours on the rig's neighbour map, so a drop's ripple runs both ways
along its strand, spreads across where strands touch, and comes back off the ends. Where two ripples meet they add or
cancel. Calm water is clear, so it sits over a bed. Reads as water, sound or a plucked string. **ripple** draws rings
from a centre in space; **wildfire** spreads light that burns out instead of waving.

## Params
- drops: 1–3 keeps a few ripples crossing; 6+ is choppy; 0 lets it settle to calm.
- speed: LEDs a second along a strand: 10–20 rolls, 40+ races.
- settle: 0.1–0.3 sloshes for seconds; 0.6+ lets each drop die out near where it fell.
- bounce: 1 keeps ripples running end to end; 0 lets them run off the ends.
- size: 2–3 LEDs is a sharp plink; 8+ long swells.

## Pairings
- Over a dim **colour-wash** in the same hues; Colours dark to bright, troughs to crests (`low high`, or an ocean pick).
- **blur** (wire) smooths the crests; **trails** leaves a wake.

## Pitfalls
- It follows the wiring: pieces the map leaves apart (floods, far arches) only move where a drop lands on them.
- A single colour shows troughs and crests the same: pick two or more.
- High drops with low settle fills the rig with chop and loses the single ripple.
