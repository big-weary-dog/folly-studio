Light catches from neighbour to neighbour on the rig's neighbour map: along each strand, across where strands touch,
and from one main arch to the next. A lit LED burns, rests, then can catch again, so fronts sweep the arches, ring the
spires and the rose, and die where they meet. Living, never repeating; reads as fire, contagion or a nervous system.
**fire** draws flames rising from an edge; **life** is a set rule per strand.

## Params
- spread: 0.2–0.4 scattered flickers that fizzle; 0.6+ fronts that run the length of an arch.
- burn and rest: their ratio sets how much is lit; rest under a second lets fires chase each other round loops.
- sparks: 1–3 keeps the rig busy; 0 lets it burn out after the first fronts.
- speed: steps a second, one LED a step: 10–20 creeps, 40+ races.
- reach: 2–3 makes fronts leap gaps in sparks and spread wider.

## Pairings
- Over a dim **colour-wash**; **trails** lengthens the embers; **blur** (wire) softens the front.
- A Colours pick dark to bright (`all` on ember or amber) reads as flame.

## Pitfalls
- It follows the wiring: pieces the map leaves apart (floods, far arches) only light by sparks.
- On a small group, raise sparks or it goes dark between fires.
