**fireworks** in gold whose **smoke** runs downward, so every burst trails off like a weeping willow, over a
near-black **colour-wash**. Shells come faster as energy rises. For celebrations and finales.

## Overrides
- smoke.drift: how fast the trails droop.
- smoke.fade: 0.6 a short crackle, 0.2 long willow tails.
- fireworks.colours: gold willow, or `all` for palette peonies.
- fireworks.speed: a number fixes the shells' pace.
- fireworks.sparks: sparks a shell.

## Pairings
- Over **brushfire** or **bonfire** for a burning finale.
- With **flash-pots** on a layer above for the hits.

## Pitfalls
- Long tails (fade below 0.2) fill the arches and lose the bursts.
