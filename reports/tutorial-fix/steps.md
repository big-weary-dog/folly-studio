# Rose and arches: each step's text

The tutorial as built, chapter by chapter, with the library's names filled in as the app shows them.
Each step's shot (1366 × 768) is linked, taken as the step is done; the old tutorial's are in `before/`. A step that
picks from the shelf (a filter, a warp, a preset) moves on as the camera comes back from the shelf's framing, so its
shot shows the next step's card.

Getting around opens with the welcome: [shot](after/first-look-welcome.png).

## Getting around

_The preview, the camera, play, and the pages._

1. **Still being built** (`report`): Studio is in development, so things will break. When they do, your name › Report a problem (or F8) tells us. [shot](after/first-look-report.png)
2. **Turn the model** (`turn`): The preview is the real rig, every LED where it hangs. Drag it to see the Folly from another side. [shot](after/first-look-turn.png)
3. **A view** (`rose`): The views put the camera on one part of the Folly in a click. Click Rose: the rose window is where our look will start. [shot](after/first-look-rose.png)
4. **Zoom** (`zoom`): Scroll on the preview to move in close to the rose window's rings. [shot](after/first-look-zoom.png)
5. **The whole Folly** (`fit`): Fit frames the whole Folly again (so does a double-click on the preview). Click Fit. [shot](after/first-look-fit.png)
6. **Pause and play** (`pause`): Pause freezes a moment to study. Click ▶ (or press Space) to pause, then again to play. [shot](after/first-look-pause.png)
7. **Ideas** (`ideas`): A look usually starts as a sentence about what you want to see. Click Ideas. [shot](after/first-look-finished.png)

## From an idea

_Describe a look; it opens in the Studio._

1. **Describe it** (`describe`): Any look can start here; the more you say about colour, motion and place, the closer the first draft. Type: "The rose window blooms open ring by ring while light runs up the main arches." [shot](after/idea-describe.png)
2. **Make it** (`make`): A model usually writes the look's code, which takes a minute; in the tutorial the look opens at once, ready-made. Click Turn it into a look. [shot](after/idea-make.png)
3. **What it made** (`layer`): Each part of a look is a layer, with its settings on the right. Rose is selected; click Arches. [shot](after/idea-layer.png)
4. **See it close** (`close`): Click Rose: the camera on the rose window and the arches round it, where the next changes show. [shot](after/idea-close.png)

## Modules and colour

_A module's numbers, choices and colours, and the palette._

1. **A number** (`speed`): Arches is drawn by Chase, a source: a module that draws light. Set its Speed (LEDs a second) to 8 or less, so the bands are easy to follow round the arch. [shot](after/modules-speed.png)
2. **A choice** (`comet`): Set Profile to Comet: each band becomes a bright head with a fading tail, so the light reads as running up the arch. [shot](after/modules-comet.png)
3. **A colour** (`high`): Colours picks from the look's palette by role. Click it and tick high: the brightest colours make the comets stand out against the rose. [shot](after/modules-high.png)
4. **The Look tab** (`look`): Settings for the whole look, not one layer, are in the Look tab. Open it. [shot](after/modules-look.png)
5. **The palette** (`palette`): Every colour in the look comes from its palette. Pick Ocean: the rose and the arches turn to deep blue and teal at once. [shot](after/modules-palette.png)

## Layers and chains

_Solo, warps and filters, where a layer plays, a preset, level, blend and order._

1. **Solo** (`solo`): Solo plays only that layer, so you can shape it without the rest. Click S on Rose. [shot](after/layers-solo.png)
2. **A filter** (`filter`): A layer's chain runs top to bottom: the source draws, then each module reworks what the ones above made. A filter changes the light itself. On Rose, click + Module, Filters, then Stained glass: the rose's panes glow like a cathedral window. [shot](after/layers-filter.png)
3. **A filter's choice** (`rule`): Set Rule to Spiral: the panes' colours wind out from the heart of the rose. [shot](after/layers-rule.png)
4. **On and off** (`switch`): A module's switch skips it and keeps its settings. Turn Stained glass off, then on again, to compare the rose with and without its glass. [shot](after/layers-onoff.png)
5. **Solo another** (`swap`): The next changes are on the arches. Click S on Rose to let it go, then S on Arches. [shot](after/layers-swap.png)
6. **A warp** (`warp`): A warp moves the light without changing its colour, between the source and the filters. On Arches, click + Module, Warps, then Rotate. [shot](after/layers-warp.png)
7. **A warp's number** (`angle`): Set Angle (turns) to 0.25 or more: at a quarter turn the comets sweep across the arches instead of up them. [shot](after/layers-angle.png)
8. **Soften** (`soften`): Soften blurs a layer at the end of its chain. Set it to 40 cm or more, so the comets' heads melt into a glow. [shot](after/layers-soften.png)
9. **Where it plays** (`plays`): A layer lights only the groups it plays on. Open Plays on and tick the small arches as well: the comets run on them too. [shot](after/layers-plays.png)
10. **Solo off** (`unsolo`): Click S on Arches again, back to the whole look. [shot](after/layers-unsolo.png)
11. **A preset** (`preset`): A preset is a layer ready-made from modules. Click + Add, Presets, then Starfield: stars glint over a dark sky on the whole Folly. [shot](after/layers-preset.png)
12. **Inside a preset** (`inside`): A preset's layer is a chain like any other, to tune. Click its Sparkle and set How many to 0.9 or more, for a sky full of stars. [shot](after/layers-inside.png)
13. **Level** (`level`): Level is how strongly a layer shows. Set the Starfield's to 50 % or less, so it is a bed for the rose and the arches. [shot](after/layers-level.png)
14. **Blend** (`blend`): Blend is how a layer meets the ones below. Pick Over: the Starfield covers them instead of adding to them, so the arches fade under it. [shot](after/layers-blend.png)
15. **Order** (`order`): What is lower is covered. Click ↓ to move the Starfield to the bottom: the rose and the arches are back, over the stars. [shot](after/layers-order.png)
16. **Mute** (`mute`): Mute silences a layer without removing it. Click M on the Starfield to see the look without it, then again to bring it back. [shot](after/layers-mute.png)

## Time

_The tempo, the beat, and a clip._

1. **The tempo** (`bpm`): BPM is the look's tempo, the one everything on the beat follows. In the Timeline, set BPM to 90. [shot](after/time-bpm.png)
2. **On the beat** (`clock`): The clock counts a speed in beats instead of seconds. On Arches, click the clock beside Speed (LEDs a second), so the comets keep time with the tempo. [shot](after/time-clock.png)
3. **A clip** (`clip`): A clip plays a layer for part of the look. With Arches selected, click + Clip: the arches now come in after the rose has opened. [shot](after/time-clip.png)

## Values that move

_Advanced: LFOs and their shapes, a macro on two values, and automation._

1. **An LFO** (`lfo`): ∿ beside a value hands it to something that moves it. An LFO is a wave. Click Starfield, its Colour wash, then the ∿ beside Brightness, and pick LFO: the sky dims and brightens by itself. [shot](after/moves-lfo.png)
2. **Its rate** (`rate`): Rate is how often the wave comes round. With a tempo it counts beats, a bar (4 beats) a wave. Set it to 16 beats or more (or pick 4 bars), so the sky breathes slowly. [shot](after/moves-rate.png)
3. **Its range** (`between`): Between is where the wave goes, from its low to its high. Set To to 0.8 or more: the sky swells bright, then sinks back to dark. [shot](after/moves-between.png)
4. **A square wave** (`square`): An LFO's shape is how it moves. On Rose, click Stained glass, the ∿ beside Amount, pick LFO, then Square: a square wave jumps between its two ends. [shot](after/moves-square.png)
5. **All the way off** (`off`): Set From to 0, so the glass snaps fully off and on again: the rose flips between plain rings and stained glass. [shot](after/moves-off.png)
6. **A saw wave** (`saw`): A saw climbs steadily and drops back. On Arches, click Rotate, the ∿ beside Angle (turns), pick LFO, then Saw: the comets' direction turns, then snaps back. [shot](after/moves-saw.png)
7. **A macro** (`macro`): A macro is one fader that can move several values together, by hand, from the timeline or from Live. In the Look tab, click + Macro. [shot](after/moves-macro.png)
8. **Tie a value to it** (`tail`): On Arches, click Chase, the ∿ beside Comet tail (comet only; LEDs), then Macro (the new macro is picked): the fader now sets how long the comets' tails are. [shot](after/moves-tail.png)
9. **Tie another** (`rings`): On Rose, click Bars, the ∿ beside Bars across the layer, then Macro: one fader now sets the arches' tails and the rose's rings together. [shot](after/moves-rings.png)
10. **Move the fader** (`fader`): In the Look tab, drag the macro to 1: long tails on the arches and many rings in the rose, at once. [shot](after/moves-fader.png)
11. **Automation** (`automate`): A lane moves a macro over time. In the Timeline, pick the macro from + Automate…, then click its lane twice: low near the start and high later, so the look builds as it plays. [shot](after/moves-automate.png)

## Keep it, show it

_Undo, save and Live._

1. **Try an idea** (`try`): Every edit is kept in History, so trying is safe. Click Arches, open Plays on and tick the whole building, to see the comets on all of the Folly. [shot](after/keep-try.png)
2. **Undo** (`undo`): Comets everywhere drown the rose. Open History and Undo (or press Ctrl+Z) until they are back on the arches. [shot](after/keep-undo.png)
3. **Save** (`save`): Save keeps Rose and arches in My looks as your own; the practice copy changed nothing of yours. Click Save. [shot](after/keep-save.png)
4. **Live** (`live`): Live is what the Folly plays now, and what is lined up next. Open it. [shot](after/keep-live.png)
